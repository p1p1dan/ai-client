   The gateway's own body is reproduced verbatim, marker included.
   Readability measured, not eyeballed: the block is
   `mb-2 max-h-28 overflow-auto rounded-md border border-destructive/40 bg-destructive/10
    px-2 py-1.5 font-mono text-code text-destructive whitespace-pre-wrap break-all`
   → font-size 12px, white-space pre-wrap, word-break break-all, max-height 112px,
   scrollHeight 47px == clientHeight 47px, scrollWidth 718 == clientWidth 718.
   Nothing is clipped and nothing needs scrolling.

Lighter observation (not counted as a defect): what the user gets is ONLY that mono block —
there is no `role="alert"` card around it, no title, no next-step sentence and no retry button,
unlike the history-error cards in item 17. Whether a bare upstream JSON body is the intended
final surface for an exhausted retry budget is a product call.
